This Privacy Policy explains how Cybor ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you play Finders Keepers (Application ID: com.cybor.finders_keepers).

1. Information Stored Locally On Your Device

Finders Keepers is built with an offline-first architecture. We do not require user accounts, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords.

Your gameplay progress is stored strictly on your local device storage using a local database (SQLite/Drift). This includes:

  • Level progression and highest unlocked level
  • In-game coin balance and hint quantities
  • Audio and gameplay preferences (sound effects, background music, haptic vibrations, color theme)
  • Daily challenge completion status

This data remains on your device and is never uploaded to any remote server operated by Cybor.

2. Third-Party Services & Analytics

To support free gameplay and provide optional rewards, Finders Keepers integrates trusted third-party advertising SDKs:

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

We display interstitial and optional rewarded video advertisements via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID) or IDFA.
  • Technical Data: Device model, operating system, network carrier, and screen dimensions.
  • Network Information: Coarse IP-based location (country or city level) to display relevant advertisements.
  • Ad Metrics: Ad impressions, view completion, and engagement data.

For more information, please review the Google Privacy Policy and Google Partner Data Usage.

In-App Purchases

If purchases are made, payments are handled directly by Google Play or Apple App Store. We do not receive, store, or process your credit card or payment information.

3. Device Permissions

  • INTERNET: Required to fetch advertisements and verify in-app purchases.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely to provide tactile haptic feedback during word selection and puzzle completion (can be disabled in Settings).

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Finders Keepers is a general audience word puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). If you believe personal information from a child has inadvertently been shared with an ad provider, please contact us immediately.

5. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

If you reside in the EEA, UK, or California, you are entitled to exercise statutory privacy rights:

  • Data Erasure: Clear your device app storage or uninstall the game at any time to delete all local progress.
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: Adjust your personalized advertising settings directly in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads) or iOS settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).
